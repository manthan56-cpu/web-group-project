About Our Institute

BrightPath Academy was established to provide solid foundational computer education. Our focus is to make sure every student understands the basics of coding, logical problem solving, and markup languages before moving on to advanced frameworks.

We believe in regular practice, small batches, and practical lab assignments rather than just theoretical memorization.

Project Guidelines Followed

Our group strictly followed all instructions specified by the faculty for this assignment:

  • Group Size: Exactly 3 students worked together in this group.
  • HTML Tags: Used standard elements including headings, paragraphs, lists, anchors, divs, and breaks.
  • HTML Tables: Designed clean tabular layouts on the courses page demonstrating rows, columns, headers, footers, row spans, and column spans.
  • HTML Forms: Built a comprehensive student registration form covering text inputs, dates, radio buttons, dropdowns, checkboxes, and buttons.
  • CSS: Applied fundamental CSS properties through an external stylesheet (colors, margins, borders, padding, font styling).
